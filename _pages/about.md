I am a research engineer at [Scaled Foundations](https://www.scaledfoundations.ai/), working on Data generation for General Robotics, Previously, I was a Research Fellow at [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-india/) focusing on alignment and optimization of language models using Reinforcement learning. My interests encompass but not limited to Representation learning and Embodied AI. My research work has been interpreting and developing data-efficient systems for General Robotics. 

In my past life I had completed my undergrad at Indian Institute of Technology (BHU), Varanasi. While, I worked with [Qin Lin](https://qinlincmu.github.io/) and [John Dolan](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/ri-faculty/john-m-dolan/) at [Argo AI Lab](https://labs.ri.cmu.edu/argo-ai-center/) at Robotics Institute, Carnegie Mellon University working on Safe Autonomous Navigation. Prior to that, I interned under [Dr. Shishir Kolathaya](https://shishirny.github.io/), IISc, bangalore at [Stochlab](https://stochlab.github.io/) on Efficient Reinforcement Learning for Quadrupeds.
